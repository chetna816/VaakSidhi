Frequently Asked Questions

What is included in VaakSiddhi Pro? +
VaakSiddhi Pro gives you unlimited daily practice across Speech-to-Text, Pronunciation Exercises, Tongue Twisters, and Public Speaking Arena. You also unlock advanced speech analysis (WPM pace, clarity, filler word counts), grammar feedback, vocabulary corrections, and personalized AI coaching.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? +
Yes! You can cancel your subscription at any time directly from your Profile or the Premium page. You will continue to have full Pro access until the end of your current billing period.
What happens after my plan expires? +
When your plan expires, your account simply reverts to the Free plan. All your earned XP, levels, badges, streaks, and speech history remain 100% saved in your account!
Will my progress and badges be saved if I downgrade? +
Yes, absolutely! Your learning progress, XP points, streaks, badges, and completed lessons are never lost when changing plans.
Are payments secure? +
Yes! All payments are processed through Razorpay's PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway supporting UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, and NetBanking.
